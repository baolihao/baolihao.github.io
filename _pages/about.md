Welcome to my homepage! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in **Applied Mathematics** at the [Illinois Institute of Technology](https://www.iit.edu/applied-math), where I am advised by [Prof. Chun Liu](https://sites.google.com/view/chunliu) and [Prof. Ming Zhong](https://mingjzhong.github.io/) (University of Houston). I received my B.Sc. in **Computing Mathematics** (First Class Honours) from [City University of Hong Kong](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/ma/), with a minor in **Computer Science**. 

My research interests lie in mathematical modeling, analysis, and scientific machine learning for complex dynamical systems, with a focus on learning governing dynamics from data, including interacting particle systems and agent- and PDE-based models arising in physical and social systems. My work emphasizes structure-preserving modeling, data-driven discovery of dynamical laws (including physics-informed neural networks), and the development of reliable numerical and analytical methods for understanding emergent collective behavior, with applications such as crime dynamics and multiscale physical systems.
