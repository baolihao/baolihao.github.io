    <td style="border:none; white-space:nowrap; vertical-align:top; padding:8px 20px 8px 0;"><strong>Research</strong></td>
    <td style="border:none; vertical-align:top; padding:8px 0;">
      My research develops mathematical and computational methods for understanding complex dynamical systems from data. I work at the intersection of <strong>scientific machine learning</strong>, <strong>inverse problems</strong>, and <strong>mathematical modeling</strong>, with a focus on three directions: (1) data-driven discovery of governing laws in interacting particle and agent-based systems; (2) physics-informed neural networks for stiff and multiscale PDEs; and (3) analysis and simulation of nonlinear PDE models arising in physical and social systems. My work combines rigorous mathematical analysis with scalable computational tools, with applications spanning urban dynamics, electrochemical systems, and power grid stability.
